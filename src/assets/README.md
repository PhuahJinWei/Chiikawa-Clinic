# Art goes here

Any file placed here with one of the names below replaces the built-in placeholder.
PNG with a transparent background; backgrounds and close-ups may be JPG instead. WebP works too,
and the rooms already here are WebP to keep the game small. If two files share a name, PNG wins.
See the project README for canvas sizes and the full lists of ids.

```
characters/chiikawa.png        (also hachiware, usagi, rakko, shisa, furuhonya,
                                momonga, kurimanju)
characters/chiikawa.fever.png  (one per trouble: fever, cold, tummy, bruise, personal)
characters/chiikawa.sit.png    (sitting: one per character, whatever the trouble)
characters/usagi.sit.personal.png  (optional: sitting with one trouble, where it shows)
characters/chiikawa.nurse.png  (in uniform while on shift; also rakko.doctor, usagi.doctor)
characters/chiikawa.happy.png  (optional: after being helped)
bg/door.day.webp               (also door.evening, door.night; any room may have these,
                                and a plain bg/door.png covers whichever is missing)
bg/waiting.webp
bg/doctor.webp
bg/counter.webp                (the wall, behind the patient)
bg/counter-shelf.webp          (the cabinet of fixes, as a layer over the wall)
fg/counter.webp                (the counter, in front of the patient)
fg/doctor.png                  (optional: the doctor's desk edge, in front of the patient)
fg/doctor-desk.webp            (the side desk behind the open chart)
fg/doctor-clipboard.webp       (the board, clip and blank paper the chart is written on)
icons/chiikawa.png             (a portrait of the head, for the round face icons)
closeups/throat.ok.webp        (also throat.red: the inside of the mouth, healthy and sore)
tools/throat.png               (the doctor's penlight)
fixes/feverMed.png             (also coldMed, stomachMed, compress, toolbox)
items/chiikawa.png             (one per character: what their personal trouble is about)
signs/door.webp                (also waiting, doctor, counter: the room pictures on the tabs,
                                choices, chart and name cards)
ui/ask.webp                    (asking: on the choice, the note strip, the chart and the guide)
ui/temp.webp                   (the thermometer, in the same places)
ui/go.webp                     (the arrow laid over a room picture on a choice that sends someone there)
bg/title.webp                  (the title screen: the clinic from outside, name on its signboard)
```
